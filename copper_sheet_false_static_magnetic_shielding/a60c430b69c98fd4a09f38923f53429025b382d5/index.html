True：A steel sphere hangs from an insulating suspension beside a fixed permanent magnet. An ordinary copper sheet slides slowly between the magnet and sphere and stops without touching either. After the insertion transient, the sphere remains deflected toward the magnet.

Counterfactual：A steel sphere hangs from an insulating suspension beside a fixed permanent magnet. An ordinary copper sheet slides slowly between the magnet and sphere and stops without touching either. Although the magnet stays fixed, the sheet wrongly eliminates its steady magnetic attraction and the sphere returns toward the vertical.
